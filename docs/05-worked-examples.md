# Worked examples

Five runs of the seven-question protocol. Confidence is H/M/L. Evidence tiers A/B/C as defined in `03-framework.md`.

---

## 1. "I bought a $400 espresso machine. I'd wanted one for ages and it was 30% off."

**Stated reason.** Long-standing desire, discount.

**Reconstruction.**
1. *Anchor + bargain signal* [L2/L4] — H, B. The 30% off against a listed price produces the mPFC "cheaper than expected" response; the absolute price is secondary.
2. *Cue-triggered wanting* [L2] — H, A. "Wanted for ages" means the cue (coffee-shop smell, Instagram kitchens, a friend's machine) has been paired repeatedly with SEEKING; the sale email was the trigger that converted standing wanting into action.
3. *Identity/status display* [L3] — M, B. A visible countertop object signals taste to guests; ask who visits.
4. *SEEKING for a project* [L1] — M, B. Machines with a learning curve recruit SEEKING (dialing in grind, latte art). The purchase is partly buying a hobby.
5. *Body state* [L0] — L. Check whether the purchase happened late at night or after a stressful day; resistance to defaults is lower when depleted.

**Feeling signature.** Valence +, arousal ↑, approach, urge moderate-high, with a brief insula "price pain" dip resolved by the discount.

**Narrative gap.** The story names L4 (deliberated) and the discount. It omits the trigger cue and the audience. "Wanted for ages" is L6's way of saying a cue had been firing for ages.

**Prediction.** Same machine, same price, no crossed-out MSRP, no email → purchase probability drops substantially. Satisfaction reports after purchase will be high regardless (expectation edits experience), so they are not evidence.

## 2. "I was full after dinner but still ate three cookies."

**Stated reason.** "They were there." Or "I deserved it."

**Reconstruction.**
1. *Sensory-specific satiety* [L2] — H, A. OFC has devalued the savory dinner; sweet, novel texture retains full value.
2. *Cue exposure* [L2] — H, A. Visible cookies on the counter are a Pavlovian cue; wanting fires independent of energy need.
3. *Habit* [L2] — M, B. If dessert follows dinner most nights, this is an overtrained context → action pair.
4. *Comfort / PANIC-GRIEF or stress* [L1/L0] — M, B. If the day was lonely or stressful, calorie-dense sweet food is a learned soother. Ask what happened that day.
5. *Hunger* [L0] — L. Stated as "full"; homeostatic drive is not the cause.

**Narrative gap.** "They were there" is unusually honest (it names the cue). "I deserved it" is L6 converting a cue-driven act into an agentic reward.

**Prediction.** Cookies in an opaque jar in another room → intake falls. Offered a fourth serving of dinner instead → declined. Loneliness manipulation raises intake of this food but not of raw vegetables.

## 3. "I met him at a concert and we just clicked. He's so genuine."

**Stated reason.** Trait: genuineness.

**Reconstruction.**
1. *Arousal misattribution* [L0/L5] — H, B. Loud music, crowd, possibly alcohol: high arousal labeled as attraction in the presence of a plausible target.
2. *Perceived similarity* [L3] — H, A. Same concert means shared taste, the kind of central attitude that produces the largest similarity effects.
3. *Fast face/voice read* [L3] — H, A. "Genuine" is the verbal translation of a 100 ms trustworthiness judgment.
4. *Reciprocity* [L3] — M, A. If he signaled interest first, that alone raises liking strongly.
5. *LUST drive state* [L1] — M. Context primes mating motives.
6. *Shared affect* [L3] — M, B. Synchronized movement and singing produce rapid felt closeness.

**Feeling signature.** Valence +, arousal ↑, approach, urge high.

**Narrative gap.** The trait word "genuine" is an L6 label for an L3 face read plus L0 arousal. The story omits the concert's role entirely.

**Prediction.** Met the same person at a DMV queue, attraction rating is lower. Liking tracks number of subsequent meetings (exposure) more than any new information about his character.

## 4. "I had a bad feeling about the candidate so we passed. Can't put my finger on it."

**Stated reason.** Gut feeling.

**Reconstruction.**
1. *Fast face/voice threat read* [L3] — H, A. Something in appearance, accent, or micro-expression triggered a low-trust judgment within the first second.
2. *Dissimilarity* [L3] — H, A. Different background, dress, or speech pattern from the interviewer's in-group; dissimilar others are encoded through a different memory route and liked less.
3. *Wicked training environment* [L4] — H, A (as a principle). Hiring gives slow, noisy, survivor-biased feedback; interviewer intuition is confident and poorly calibrated here per Kahneman & Klein.
4. *Interviewer body state* [L0] — M. Late-afternoon interviews, hunger, fatigue → harsher judgments.
5. *Error asymmetry* [evolutionary] — M. False-positive trust historically costlier than a missed ally; the system is biased to reject under uncertainty.

**Narrative gap.** "Can't put my finger on it" correctly reports that L6 has no access. The error is treating inaccessibility as a mark of validity.

**Prediction.** Structured scoring on work samples, blinded to appearance, will disagree with the gut call at a rate near chance. Interviewer's "bad feeling" rate rises after lunch-skipping and in late slots.

## 5. "I'm so anxious about this launch." (Said by someone who is also excited.)

**Stated reason.** Anxiety about an event.

**Reconstruction.**
1. *Core affect* [L0] — H, A. High arousal, valence ambiguous. The body state for "anxious" and "excited" is largely the same.
2. *Label supplied by context* [L5] — H, A. Uncertainty + stakes → the culture's default word is "anxious".
3. *Drive mix* [L1] — M. FEAR (reputational threat) and SEEKING (novelty, potential) both active; the label picks one.
4. *Sleep and caffeine* [L0] — M. Launch weeks raise both arousal sources; some of the "anxiety" is pharmacological.
5. *Impact bias* [L5] — H, A. The forecast intensity and duration of post-launch feelings (good or bad) are overestimated.

**Translation.** Arousal ↑, valence mixed, FEAR + SEEKING, context-labeled. Relabeling to "excited" (same body, different concept) measurably improves performance in the literature on reappraisal.

**Prediction.** Reduce caffeine and restore sleep → reported anxiety drops with no change in the launch. Ask "what are you looking forward to?" → the same arousal gets relabeled within the conversation.
