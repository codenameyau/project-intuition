# Evidence and sources

Annotated bibliography grouped by framework layer. Each entry carries an evidence tier:

- **A** replicated and mechanistic, or a large meta-analysis
- **B** well supported but single-lab, correlational, or small-N imaging
- **C** contested, failed replication, or theory ahead of data

Entries marked *(secondary)* were confirmed only through abstracts, reviews, or press summaries during the sweep; the primary paper should be read before relying on a specific number.
