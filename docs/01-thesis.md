# Thesis: what intuition is, in one page

Most human decisions are not reasoned. They are *felt* and then *narrated*. This project treats that as a mechanism to be modeled, not a flaw to be lamented.

## The claim

Intuition is the output of a stack of fast, parallel, body-anchored prediction systems. Those systems were shaped over evolutionary time (phylogeny) and tuned by the individual's lived experience (ontogeny). Their output reaches awareness as a **feeling** with a signature (pleasant/unpleasant, calm/activated, approach/avoid, urge strength), not as an argument. The verbal "reason" a person gives afterwards is produced by a *separate* narrating system that has only partial access to the real causes.

So, to explain why someone did, bought, ate, liked, or felt something, you do **not** ask what they said. You reconstruct the drivers underneath, then compare the reconstruction to the story they told. The gap between the two is itself informative.

## Six load-bearing findings

1. **The body comes first.** The brain's primary job is regulating the body's energy budget (allostasis). Interoceptive signals from the gut, heart, lungs, and skin are integrated in the insula and read out as core affect: valence and arousal. Hunger, fatigue, and arousal state change what looks like a good idea before any "thinking" occurs. (Barrett; Craig; Seth)
2. **Ancient drives are built in.** Electrical stimulation of subcortical circuits produces the same seven motivational states across mammals: SEEKING, FEAR, RAGE, LUST, CARE, PANIC/GRIEF, PLAY. Decorticate animals still show them. Add DISGUST (the behavioral immune system) and you have the motivational alphabet every intuition is spelled from. (Panksepp; Tybur; Schaller)
3. **Wanting is not liking.** Dopamine encodes a prediction error that teaches the brain what to expect and attaches "incentive salience" to cues. Cues can trigger wanting without pleasure, and below awareness. This is why cravings, impulse buys, and "I don't know why I did that" exist. (Schultz; Berridge; Pessiglione)
4. **The brain is a prediction machine.** Perception and action are driven by top-down predictions corrected by error. Expertise is compressed prediction learned in environments with fast, honest feedback. Intuition is trustworthy exactly when the environment that trained it was regular and the feedback was clear. (Friston; Clark; Kahneman & Klein; Hogarth)
5. **Social evaluation is instantaneous.** Trustworthiness, attractiveness, and competence judgments stabilize within 100 ms of seeing a face. Similarity, familiarity, proximity, and reciprocity predict liking with large effect sizes. Observing another's disgust activates your own insula. (Willis & Todorov; Montoya; Bornstein; Wicker)
6. **The explainer is not the decider.** Split-brain patients confidently invent reasons for actions caused by the other hemisphere. Intact people give fluent reasons for choices they never made (choice blindness), and defend moral judgments they cannot justify (moral dumbfounding). Stated reasons are reconstructions. (Gazzaniga; Nisbett & Wilson; Johansson; Haidt)

## What this lets you do

Given any situation (a purchase, a snack, a crush, a grudge, a "gut feeling"), run a fixed procedure that asks: What was the body state? Which drive was active? What cues were present and what had they been paired with? What did the person's model predict? Who was watching? Which error was evolutionarily costlier? What story did they tell, and where does it diverge from the mechanism?

The output is an explanation that is **mechanistic**, **ranked by confidence**, **tagged by evidence quality**, and **falsifiable**: it should predict what would change the behavior.

## What this is not

It is not a claim that people are irrational. Most intuitions are adaptive in the environments that produced them. It is not a claim that reasoning never matters; deliberate control does intervene, but late, rarely, and mostly when the fast systems disagree with each other. And it is not settled science. Several pillars are contested, and the sources document says which.
